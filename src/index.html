What the PAC does

Parents helping McRoberts students, one year at a time

  • We fund clubs, teams, trips and events through the Teachers' Wish List.
  • We support the Grade 12 Dry After Grad celebration.
  • We raise money for Grade 12 scholarships.
  • We say thank you to McRoberts staff.
  • We share parents' voices with the school and the Richmond school district.
