How to sign up

  1. Choose a job

    Pick one from the list above, or more than one.

  2. Email the PAC

    Use a button on a job card, or the button below. Tell us your name and which job you would like.

  3. We get in touch

    A PAC volunteer replies with the details, like the date and time.

Join the executive

The executive is a small team of parent volunteers. Parents elect them every year at the first PAC meeting in September. If you are thinking about it, talk to us any time before then.

What each executive role does
RoleWhat the job is
ChairCalls and runs meetings, speaks for the PAC, and reads the PAC's letters and email.
SecretaryWrites the meeting minutes and PAC letters.
TreasurerKeeps the money records and reports the account balances at each meeting.
RDPA RepresentativeGoes to meetings of the Richmond District Parents Association (RDPA) and reports back.

The PAC also has Co-Chairs, a Co-Treasurer, a Fundraising lead and a Dry After Grad Chair who share the work.

Where possible, one person holds a role for up to two years, then helps the next person learn it. The RDPA runs "PAC 101" workshops for executive members.
