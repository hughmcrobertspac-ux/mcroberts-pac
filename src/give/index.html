Ways to give

Help pay for what the grant cannot

The Province's grant pays for clubs, teams and trips. It cannot pay for Grade 12 scholarships or for thanking staff. Family fundraisers and donations pay for those.

In short

Buy from a PAC fundraiser, give an hour of your time, or donate when donations open. The money pays for scholarships and staff thank-yous. Giving is always optional.

Why families fundraise

Each year the PAC can apply to the Province of BC for a grant of $20 for each student. The grant pays for clubs, teams, field trips and events that help students outside regular class time.

The grant has strict rules. It cannot pay for scholarships. It cannot pay for gifts, food or events for teachers and staff. Only money from families can pay for those.

Goals for {{site.schoolYear}}

Family support pays for two things the grant cannot.

  • Scholarships for graduates. The PAC gives scholarships of $500 to graduating students. The goal for this year is 5 scholarships, or $2,500 in total. Last year, families raised enough for 3.
  • Thanking our staff. Each June the PAC holds a breakfast to thank McRoberts teachers and staff. Parent volunteers set it up and serve. Families pay for it, because the grant cannot.
