Questions about your own child

The PAC does not handle questions or concerns about one student. Please contact the school first.

Start with the person closest to the question. If you still need help, go to the next step.

  1. Your child's teacher

    For questions about a class, homework or marks.

  2. Your child's counsellor

    If you still need help after talking to the teacher.

  3. The vice principal or principal

    If the question is still not answered.

  4. The school district

    The last step, if the school cannot solve it.
