In short

The school cannot give the PAC your email address. To get PAC emails, send the PAC one email and you are on the list. You can stop at any time.

Why you need to sign up

The school sends emails to every family. The PAC cannot do the same. The PAC is a separate group of parent volunteers, and privacy rules do not allow the school to share parents' email addresses with it.

So the PAC can only email families who ask. This page is where you ask.

How to join

  1. Tap the button

    Your email app opens a new message to the PAC email list. The address and subject are already filled in.

  2. Send it, then confirm once

    You do not need to write anything else. You will get one confirmation email; reply to it (or tap its confirm link) to prove the address is yours.

  3. You are on the list

    The list adds your address by itself. No volunteer types it in, and you get the next PAC email.

Join the PAC email list

No email app on this device? Write from your usual email to {{site.emailList.subscribe}}

The PAC only needs your email address. It does not need your child's name or grade.

What we send

PAC emails will cover meeting reminders, fundraisers, volunteer jobs and short updates after meetings. Expect about one or two emails a month during the school year.

Every email will say it is from the McRoberts PAC and tell you how to stop getting PAC emails.

Your email address is used only for PAC news. The PAC never sells it or shares it. Read the privacy notice.

Stop at any time

Changed your mind? Send an email to the list's leave address and confirm once, and you are off the list. No volunteer needed, and you do not need to give a reason.
