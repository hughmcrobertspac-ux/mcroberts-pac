Accessibility statement

A website every family can use

The PAC wants every McRoberts family to be able to read and use this site, on any device and in the way that works best for them.

In short

We aim to meet the WCAG 2.2 AA standard. Some parts are still being tested. If anything on the site is hard to use, email us and we will help.

Our goal

This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. WCAG is the international standard for accessible websites. Level AA is the usual target for websites in Canada.

What we have done

Keyboard

You can use the whole site with a keyboard. The first link on every page skips to the main content. A bright yellow outline shows where you are.

Contrast

Text and background colours were chosen to meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, in both the light and the dark theme. Nothing depends on colour alone.

Less motion

If your device is set to reduce motion, the site turns off its animations. Nothing flashes, and nothing plays by itself.

Light and dark

The site follows your device's light or dark setting. You can also choose Auto, Light or Dark at the bottom of every page.

Bigger text

You can zoom in to 200 percent. The layout is built to fit small phone screens without scrolling sideways.

Plain language

We write in plain English with short sentences. Most pages start with an "In short" summary. Dates and times are written as text, not only in calendar files.

Every page is marked as English, so screen readers can use the right voice. Pages use clear headings in order, tables have headers, and there is no text inside pictures.

Known gaps in this preview

This site is a draft. These parts are not finished yet.

  • Screen reader testing is still to do. We have not yet tested the site with screen readers such as VoiceOver and NVDA. We will do this before the site launches.
  • Older browsers. On some older browsers, the Menu button may not open. Every page is also listed at the bottom of each page.
  • Documents on other sites. Meeting minutes and agendas are on the school website. We have not checked each one for accessibility.
  • "TBC" labels. Some facts are marked TBC while the PAC executive confirms them. Screen readers also read a short note that says what still needs to be confirmed.

Tell us about a barrier

If something on this site is hard to see, read or use, please email us. Tell us:

  • which page you were on
  • what you were trying to do
  • what went wrong, and which device or browser you used, if you know

We will try to fix it. We can also send you the information in another way, for example as plain text in an email. We are parent volunteers, and we will reply as soon as we can.
