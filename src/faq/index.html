The PAC and you

What is the PAC?

PAC means Parent Advisory Council. At McRoberts, the PAC is a group of parents and guardians. It supports students, works with the principal and staff, and shares parents' views with the school and the Richmond school district.

The PAC is run by parent volunteers. It is separate from the school. More about the PAC

Do I need to sign up or pay to be a member?

You do not need to sign up. If your child goes to McRoberts, you are already a member.

There is no membership fee.

Who can vote?

Parents and guardians of McRoberts students can vote at PAC meetings. School staff and community members are welcome to come, but they do not vote.

How is the PAC executive chosen?

Parents and guardians elect the executive every year at the first PAC meeting in September. The executive are parent volunteers.

Would you like to join the executive? Talk to the PAC any time before September. Meet this year's executive

Money

Where does the PAC's money come from?

From three places:

  • a yearly grant from the Province of BC, $20 for each student
  • family fundraisers, like food and chocolate sales
  • donations from families

See where the money goes

If the PAC gets a grant, why does it still fundraise?

The Province's grant has strict rules. It cannot pay for Grade 12 scholarships. It cannot pay for thanking teachers and staff. Only money from families can pay for these.

Ways to give

Who decides how PAC money is spent?

Parents do. Spending is voted on at PAC meetings. Before a vote, the executive checks each request against the grant rules.

Teachers ask for money through the Teachers' Wish List each fall. The principal collects their requests, and parents vote on them at a meeting. How the Wish List works

Can I see the PAC's finances?

Yes. The treasurer gives a report at every PAC meeting. The yearly financial report is on this site: the 2025–26 report is a draft until the Treasurer and the PAC executive approve it.

Words you will hear

What is Dry After Grad?

A safe, alcohol-free party for Grade 12 graduates. It happens after the grad dinner and dance. A committee of Grade 12 parents plans it, and in 2025–26 the PAC gave about $10 for every graduate.

More about Dry After Grad

What is the RDPA?

The Richmond District Parents Association. It brings together PACs from across Richmond and works with the Richmond School District (SD38). The McRoberts PAC has an RDPA representative, who reports back at PAC meetings.

BCCPAC, the BC Confederation of Parent Advisory Councils, does the same for all of BC.

What is PLT?

PLT means Personal Learning Time. It happens on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Students can work on their own or get help from a teacher. They sign up the day before.

Read about PLT on the school website

Getting help

I have a concern about my child's class. Can the PAC help?

Please talk to your child's teacher first. If that does not solve it, talk to the counsellor, then a vice principal or the principal. The PAC does not handle concerns about one student.

The PAC is the place for ideas and concerns that affect many students. You can raise them at a PAC meeting or by email. Who to contact for what
