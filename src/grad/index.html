Dry After Grad

A safe, alcohol-free celebration for McRoberts graduates on grad night. Grade 12 parents plan it, and the PAC helps pay for it.

In short

A safe party for graduates after the grad dinner, planned by Grade 12 parents. In 2025–26 the PAC gave about $10 for every graduate. If your child is in Grade 12, the committee needs your help.

What it is

The party happens after the grad dinner and dance, so graduates can keep celebrating together in a safe place.

The Grade 12 parent committee that plans it is part of the PAC, and its chair reports at PAC meetings. The committee raises its own money and has its own bank account.

How the PAC helps

In 2025–26 the PAC gave Dry After Grad about $10 for every graduate, $2,040 in total, from the BC gaming grant. The Province's rules allow the grant to pay for graduation events, including dry grad.

The PAC paid it in August 2026, with its wish-list cheque to the school. It paid for the bus on grad night.

The committee raises the rest through its own fundraisers and ticket sales.
