First time? What to expect

You can just listen. You do not have to speak, and you do not have to come to every meeting. Come when you can.

A meeting takes about one hour. There is a written agenda, and the Chair leads the meeting. Most meetings follow these steps:

  1. Welcome

    The Chair opens the meeting and welcomes everyone.

  2. Agenda and last minutes

    Parents approve the agenda and the minutes of the last meeting.

  3. Principal's report

    The principal talks about what is happening at school and answers questions.

  4. Reports

    The Treasurer, Secretary, Dry After Grad committee, Fundraising team and RDPA Representative give short updates.

  5. Business and votes

    Parents talk about PAC plans and vote on things like spending. Only parents and guardians vote.

  6. Questions and next meeting

    Time for your questions and ideas, then the date of the next meeting.

Sometimes a guest speaker comes too. In 2025–26, the school librarians and Canadian Parents for French spoke at PAC meetings. The September meeting also includes the elections.

Language

Meetings are in English. If you would like help in another language, email the PAC before the meeting to ask.

How decisions are made

  • Only parents and guardians of McRoberts students vote. School staff and community members are welcome, but they do not vote.
  • To make decisions, a meeting needs a quorum: at least two-thirds of the executive and at least five members.
  • PAC spending is voted on at meetings.
  • Meetings follow Robert's Rules of Order, a common set of rules for running fair meetings.
  • The PAC can also hold a special meeting. The principal and parents get seven days' notice, except when the matter is urgent.

Elections every September

The executive is elected every year at the first meeting of the school year, in September. The previous Chair, or the principal, runs the election. The 2026–27 executive was elected on September 16, 2026.

Would you like to join the executive next year? Talk to the PAC any time before September.
