Who to contact for what

For a question about your own child, start with the person who knows your child best. If that does not solve it, go to the next step.

  1. Your child's teacher

    For questions about a class, marks or homework.

  2. Your child's counsellor

    Each student has a counsellor, based on their last name, listed on the school's staff page. A counsellor can help when a concern is bigger than one class.

  3. A vice principal or the principal

    If the teacher and counsellor cannot solve it, ask the school office for a vice principal or the principal.

  4. The Richmond School District

    If the school cannot solve it, contact the Richmond School District (SD38).

Not sure where to start? Call the school office at 604-668-6600 or email mcroberts@sd38.bc.ca. The office can point you to the right person.

When to contact the PAC

The PAC is a group of parent volunteers, separate from the school. Come to the PAC for things that affect many students and families.

Parents' views and meetings

Bring ideas and concerns that affect many students to a PAC meeting, or email the PAC. The PAC shares parents' views with the principal and the Richmond District Parents Association.
