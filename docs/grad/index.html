Dry After Grad

A safe, alcohol-free celebration for McRoberts graduates on grad night. Grade 12 parents plan it, and the PAC helps pay for it.

In short

Dry After Grad is a safe party for graduates after the grad dinner. A committee of Grade 12 parents plans it. In 2025–26 the PAC gave about $10 for every graduate. If your child is in Grade 12, the committee needs your help.

What it is

Dry After Grad is a safe, alcohol-free party for Grade 12 graduates. It happens after the grad dinner and dance, so graduates can keep celebrating together in a safe place.

A committee of Grade 12 parents plans it. The committee is part of the McRoberts PAC, and its chair reports at PAC meetings. The committee raises its own money and has its own bank account.

How the PAC helps

In 2025–26 the PAC gave Dry After Grad about $10 for every graduate, $2,040 in total. This money comes from the BC gaming grant. The Province's rules allow the grant to pay for graduation events, including dry grad.

The PAC paid it in August 2026, as part of its wish-list cheque to the school. It paid for the bus on grad night.

The committee raises the rest through its own fundraisers and ticket sales.

Class of 2026

210 students graduated. The grad dinner and Dry After Grad were on June 22 and June 23, 2026.

The dinner was at a golf and country club close to the school. The committee raised money with ice-cream sandwich sales and other events. Thank you to every Grade 12 parent who helped.
