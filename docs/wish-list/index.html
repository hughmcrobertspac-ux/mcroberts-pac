Teachers' Wish List

Each fall, McRoberts teachers and coaches ask the PAC for money for things students need outside regular class time. Parents decide together what to fund.

In short

Staff send requests through the principal in the fall. The PAC executive checks them against the grant rules. Parents vote at a PAC meeting. The school buys the items, and the PAC pays the school back.

How it works

Wish List money comes from the BC gaming grant. Every request goes through five steps.

  1. Ask in the fall

    Teachers, coaches and club sponsors send requests through the principal, using an online form. Each request says which group it is for, what is needed, how much it costs, and how it helps students.

  2. Check the rules

    The PAC executive checks each request against the Province's rules for the grant.

  3. Parents vote

    At a PAC meeting, parents talk about each request and vote. Any McRoberts parent or guardian can vote.

  4. The school buys

    The school orders and pays for the approved items.

  5. The PAC pays the school back

    From the gaming account, usually with one cheque for the whole list.

When to ask

Requests are collected in the fall. Parents usually vote in October or November.

2026–27. The principal is collecting requests from teachers and clubs now. Parents will look at them at the PAC meeting on November 18, 2026.

If money is left after the fall vote, the PAC can sometimes fund a few last-minute requests later in the year.

What the money can pay for

Every request must follow the Province's gaming grant rules. The grant is for activities outside regular class time.

Can pay for

  • Clubs and teams
  • Uniforms and equipment for teams, clubs and extracurricular sports
  • Field trips within BC
  • Guest speakers, presentations and performances
  • Student competitions, and student conferences in BC
  • Graduation events, including Dry After Grad
  • School spirit events
  • Certificates, trophies and other student awards
  • Books, magazines and games for free time

Cannot pay for

  • Textbooks and other things used to teach the curriculum
  • Tablets, laptops, Chromebooks or smart boards for class
  • Special equipment for elective courses, like lights or props for drama class
  • Things the school or school district must provide
  • Building repairs
  • Costs for teachers and staff
  • Scholarships
  • Cash prizes and gift cards
  • Field trips outside BC

See the full lists on Where the money goes. Not sure about an item? Try the Province's decision tree for PAC grant purchases (PDF, 370 KB) (opens in a new tab), or ask the PAC.

Questions

  • Staff: ask the principal about the request form, or email the PAC.
  • Students: if you have an idea, ask a teacher or club sponsor to send a request for your group.
  • Parents: come to the meeting where parents vote. See the meeting dates.
