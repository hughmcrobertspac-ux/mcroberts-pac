New to McRoberts

Questions from new families

Short answers to the questions parents ask most. New to McRoberts, or starting Grade 8? Start here.

In short

If your child goes to McRoberts, you are already a PAC member. You can come to any PAC meeting, in person or online, and just listen.

The PAC and you

What is the PAC?

PAC means Parent Advisory Council. It is a group of McRoberts parents and guardians, run by volunteers and separate from the school.

It supports students, works with the principal and staff, and shares parents' views with the school and the Richmond school district. More about the PAC

Do I need to sign up or pay to be a member?

No. If your child goes to McRoberts, you are already a member, and there is no fee.

Who can vote?

Parents and guardians of McRoberts students can vote at PAC meetings. School staff and community members are welcome but do not vote.

How is the PAC executive chosen?

Parents and guardians elect the executive, who are parent volunteers, at the first PAC meeting each September.

Want to join? Talk to the PAC any time before September. Meet this year's executive

Meetings

Do I have to come to every meeting?

No. Come when you can. You can just listen.

Meetings start at 7:00 pm and take about one hour. See all meeting dates

Can I join a meeting online?

Yes. Each meeting is in the School Library and on Microsoft Teams at the same time. Join the meeting on Microsoft Teams using the link below.

Join online on Microsoft Teams

What language are meetings in?

Meetings are in English. For help in another language, email the PAC before the meeting and we will see what we can do.

Money

Where does the PAC's money come from?

From three places:

  • a yearly grant from the Province of BC, $20 for each student
  • family fundraisers, like food and chocolate sales
  • donations from families

See where the money goes

If the PAC gets a grant, why does it still fundraise?

The Province's grant has strict rules. It cannot pay for Grade 12 scholarships or for thanking teachers and staff. Only money from families can.

Ways to give

Who decides how PAC money is spent?

Parents do, by voting at PAC meetings. Before a vote, the executive checks each request against the grant rules.

Each fall, teachers ask for money through the Teachers' Wish List. The principal collects the requests for the parents' vote. How the Wish List works

Can I see the PAC's finances?

Yes. The treasurer reports at every PAC meeting, and the yearly financial report is on this site. The Treasurer reviewed the 2025–26 report in October 2026.

Words you will hear

What is Dry After Grad?

A safe, alcohol-free party for Grade 12 graduates, after the grad dinner and dance. Grade 12 parents plan it. In 2025–26 the PAC gave about $10 for every graduate.

More about Dry After Grad

What is the RDPA?

The Richmond District Parents Association. It brings together PACs from across Richmond and works with the Richmond School District (SD38). The PAC's RDPA representative reports back at PAC meetings.

BCCPAC, the BC Confederation of Parent Advisory Councils, does the same for all of BC.

What is PLT?

PLT means Personal Learning Time, on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Students work on their own or get help from a teacher. They sign up the day before.

Read about PLT on the school website

Getting help

I have a concern about my child's class. Can the PAC help?

Talk to your child's teacher first, then the counsellor, then a vice principal or the principal. The PAC does not handle concerns about one student.

The PAC is the place for ideas and concerns that affect many students. Raise them at a PAC meeting or by email. Who to contact for what
