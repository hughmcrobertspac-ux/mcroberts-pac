First time? What to expect

You can just listen. You do not have to speak or come to every meeting.

There is a written agenda, and the Chair leads. Most meetings follow these steps:

  1. Welcome

    The Chair opens the meeting and welcomes everyone.

  2. Agenda and last minutes

    Parents approve the agenda and the minutes of the last meeting.

  3. Principal's report

    The principal talks about what is happening at school and answers questions.

  4. Reports

    The Treasurer, Secretary, Dry After Grad committee, Fundraising team and RDPA Representative give short updates.

  5. Business and votes

    Parents talk about PAC plans and vote on things like spending.

  6. Questions and next meeting

    Time for your questions and ideas, then the date of the next meeting.

Sometimes a guest speaker comes. In 2025–26, the school librarians and Canadian Parents for French spoke. The September meeting also holds the elections.

Coming in person

Meetings are in the School Library at Hugh McRoberts Secondary, 8980 Williams Road, Richmond, BC.

Joining online

Meetings are also on Microsoft Teams. Email hughmcrobertspac@gmail.com and ask for the link.

Language

Meetings are in English. For help in another language, email the PAC before the meeting.

How decisions are made

  • Only parents and guardians of McRoberts students vote. School staff and community members are welcome but do not vote.
  • To make decisions, a meeting needs a quorum: at least two-thirds of the executive and at least five members.
  • PAC spending is voted on at meetings.
  • Meetings follow Robert's Rules of Order, a common set of rules for running fair meetings.
  • A special meeting needs seven days' notice to the principal and parents, unless the matter is urgent.

Elections every September

The executive is elected at the first meeting of each school year, in September. The previous Chair, or the principal, runs the election. The 2026–27 executive was elected on September 16, 2026.

Want to join the executive next year? Talk to the PAC any time before September.
