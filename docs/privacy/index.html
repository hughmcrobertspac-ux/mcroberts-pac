Privacy notice

Your privacy

The McRoberts PAC follows BC's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). This page explains what personal information the PAC has, why it has it, and how it is protected.

In short

This website does not track you and has no forms. The PAC only has what you choose to email us. We never sell it or share it, and you can ask to see or correct it at any time.

Who is responsible

The Hugh McRoberts Secondary Parent Advisory Council (the PAC) is responsible for the personal information it collects. The PAC is run by parent volunteers. It is separate from the school and the Richmond School District. They follow a different privacy law (FOIPPA) for the information they hold.

The PAC's privacy contact is the PAC SecretaryTBC (to be confirmed: The executive still has to choose the privacy contact: the Secretary or the Chair). You can reach the privacy contact at the PAC email. Please write "Privacy" in the subject line.

hughmcrobertspac@gmail.com

This website does not track you

  • It sets no cookies.
  • It uses no analytics, no ads and no tracking tools.
  • It loads nothing from other companies. Even the fonts are stored on this site.
  • It has no forms and no accounts. Nothing you do here is sent to the PAC.

Your browser may remember one small setting on your own device. If you choose Light or Dark at the bottom of a page, your browser remembers that choice. This setting is never sent to the PAC, and you can clear it in your browser.

While this site is a preview, it is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub keeps basic technical records of visits, such as IP addresses, for security. The PAC cannot see these records.

If the PAC ever wants to count visits to the site, it will use a tool that sets no cookies and collects no personal information. It will update this page first.

Links to other websites, such as the school or the Province, take you to sites with their own privacy rules.

What we collect and why

The PAC only has the personal information that you choose to send by email to hughmcrobertspac@gmail.com. That can include:

  • your name and email address
  • the language you prefer, if you tell us
  • the volunteer jobs you are interested in
  • your question or message

We use it to:

  • answer your question
  • send you the online link for a PAC meeting
  • send you PAC news, if you ask for it
  • connect you with a volunteer job

We only ask for what we need. Please do not email us private details about your child, such as health information or marks. We do not need them.

If you pay the PAC by Interac e-Transfer, for example for a fundraiser order, the payment shows your name and email address. We use it to match your payment to your order and to keep the PAC's money records.

PAC fundraiser order forms are separate from this website. Each form will tell you what it collects, why, who sees it and how long it is kept.TBC (to be confirmed: The executive still has to adopt this rule for PAC forms)

Your consent

When you email the PAC, you agree that we can use your information to do what you asked. We will not use it for anything else unless we ask you first.

You can change your mind at any time. Email us and tell us what to stop, for example "please stop sending me PAC news". If you withdraw your consent, we may not be able to do some things for you, such as send you the online meeting link.

How we use and share it

  • We never sell your information.
  • We never share it with businesses, sponsors or other groups.
  • We never add you to a list you did not ask to join.
  • The school does not give parents' contact details to the PAC. We never ask for school or class lists.
  • We share information only when the law requires it.

Where it is kept, and for how long

The PAC email account is a Gmail account, run by Google. Google may store email on servers outside Canada, for example in the United States. The laws of that country may let its authorities see the information.

No personal information is stored on this website.

We keep information only as long as we need it:

  • Emails, including volunteer sign-ups: deleted after the school year ends, unless we still need them to help you.
  • Information we used to make a decision about you: kept for at least one year, as PIPA requires, and then deleted.
  • PAC news: if you ask to stop, we remove your address right away.
  • Payment records: kept with the PAC's financial records for as long as the rules for PAC money require, and then deleted.

How we keep it safe

  • Personal information never goes on this website.
  • Only executive members who need it can open the PAC email account.TBC (to be confirmed: The executive has to confirm who can open the PAC mailbox)
  • The PAC email account is protected with two-step sign-in.
  • When we email many families at once, we hide everyone's email address.TBC (to be confirmed: The executive has to confirm how group emails are sent)
  • Money is shown on this site only as totals. We never show account numbers, cheques, signatures or the names of donors.

If something goes wrong

If personal information is lost, stolen or seen by someone who should not see it, the PAC will take these steps.

  1. Stop it

    We act right away to stop the problem and get the information back if we can.

  2. Find out what happened

    We check what information was involved and who is affected.

  3. Tell the people affected

    We tell you as soon as we can, and what you can do to protect yourself. If there is a real risk of serious harm, we also tell the OIPC (see below).

  4. Stop it happening again

    We change how we work so it does not happen again.

Photos and what we publish

Photos of people are personal information. There are no photos on this website now. If the PAC adds photos later, these rules apply:

  • No photos of students.
  • A photo of any person goes on the site only with that person's written permission.
  • The school's photo permission form (media release) does not cover the PAC. Saying yes to the school is not saying yes to the PAC.

The PAC also follows these rules for everything on this site:

  • Executive members are named only with their permission. Their personal emails and phone numbers never appear.
  • No names of students. Parents are named only if they are on the executive and agree to it.
  • Only approved meeting minutes, never drafts.
  • No discussion of any one student, parent or staff member.

See or correct your information

You can ask to see the personal information the PAC has about you. If something is wrong, you can ask us to correct it.

  • Email the PAC with "Privacy request" in the subject line. Say what you would like to see or change.
  • We may ask you to confirm who you are, so we do not give your information to someone else.
  • In most cases we will answer within 30 business days, as PIPA requires.
Email a privacy request

Questions and complaints

If you have a question or a concern about how the PAC handles personal information, please email the privacy contact first. We will try to fix it.

If you are not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia (OIPC). The OIPC is independent. It makes sure organizations follow PIPA.

Visit the OIPC BC website (oipc.bc.ca)

Changes to this notice

We update this notice when the way the PAC handles information changes. The executive reviews it every September.TBC (to be confirmed: The executive still has to agree to a yearly review)
