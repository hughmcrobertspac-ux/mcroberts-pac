About this report

This report shows where PAC money came from and where it went in the 2025–26 school year. Most of it came from the BC Community Gaming Grant. The Province gives for each student every year, for activities outside regular classes. The grant cannot pay for scholarships or for thanking our staff. Those depend on you, through fundraisers and donations.

Thank you to every family who ordered samosas, chocolates and bubble tea or gave toward the staff breakfast, to the volunteers who ran each sale, and to last year's executive and treasurers, whose records made this report possible. This report's figures for the gaming account and the family fund are checked against the PAC's bank record for the whole year.

How PAC money works

Two accounts, two jobs

The PAC keeps two bank accounts at Coast Capital Savings. Each one has its own source of money and its own rules. This report covers both for the whole school year. Dry After Grad has its own committee and its own account; the PAC supports it from the gaming account.

!

The grant has limits. Provincial rules do not allow gaming money to pay for scholarships, staff lunches or anything the school must provide for classes. The family fund pays for the things the grant cannot.

What families made possible
