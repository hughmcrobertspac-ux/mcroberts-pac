Parent Advisory Council

About the PAC

PAC means Parent Advisory Council. Parent volunteers run it. It supports students, works with the school and shares parents' views.

In short

If your child goes to McRoberts, you are a PAC member. You do not need to sign up. Parents and guardians meet, vote on how PAC money is spent, and give the school their ideas.

What the PAC is

The BC School Act lets the parents of each public school form one Parent Advisory Council. This is the PAC of Hugh McRoberts Secondary in Richmond.

Every parent or guardian of a McRoberts student is a member, with no fee.

Why the PAC exists

  • To help parents take part in their children's education.
  • To support staff so students do well.
  • To help the school community talk, respect each other and work together.
  • To give parents a place to talk and get involved.

The bylaws' own words are in Our bylaws, below.

What the PAC does

  • Meets. About seven times a year. The principal reports and answers parents' questions. See the meeting dates.
  • Funds student activities. A yearly Province of BC grant pays for the clubs, teams, trips and events that teachers ask for through the Teachers' Wish List. Parents vote on the list.
  • Raises money. Family fundraisers pay for what the grant cannot, like Grade 12 scholarships and thanking staff. Ways to give.
  • Supports Dry After Grad. The PAC helps pay for this safe, alcohol-free celebration for graduates. About Dry After Grad.
  • Speaks up. The PAC shares parents' views with the school and the Richmond District Parents Association (RDPA). In February 2026, it gathered parents' budget views for an RDPA survey. Parents put students' learning needs, with enough teachers and staff, first.
  • Welcomes new families. The PAC wants new families, especially in Grade 8, to feel at home.

Members and voting

Can vote

  • Parents and guardians of McRoberts students

Welcome, but cannot vote

  • The principal and vice principals
  • Teachers and other school staff
  • Community members who do not have a child at McRoberts

Come to as many meetings as you can. You can just listen.

Giving advice to the school

Under section 8 of the BC School Act, a PAC may, through its elected officers, advise the school board, the principal and staff on any matter about the school.

Most of this happens at PAC meetings. The principal, Mr. Jason Leslie, or a staff member, comes to every meeting to report and answer questions. In 2025–26, parents asked about course changes, a university fair and student competitions.

Richmond School District rules (Regulation 802.4-R) say a PAC should support the principal's leadership. PAC meetings do not discuss individual staff, students or parents.

The PAC does not handle concerns about one student. For a question about your own child, contact the school first. Who to contact at the school.

Independent from the school

The PAC works closely with the school, but it is a separate group run by parent volunteers. This is not an official website of Hugh McRoberts Secondary or Richmond School District No. 38.

For school business, attendance or records, contact the school office at 604-668-6600 or mcroberts@sd38.bc.ca, or visit the McRoberts school website.

What the main roles do

  • Chair: calls and runs meetings, speaks for the PAC, reviews letters and emails to the PAC, and appoints committee members.
  • Secretary: writes the minutes and drafts letters.
  • Treasurer: keeps the financial records, reports the account balances at each meeting, and follows school board rules for PAC funds.
  • RDPA Representative: goes to district parent meetings and reports back.

Want to join the executive? Elections are at the first meeting each September. Talk to the PAC any time before then.

Our bylaws

The constitution and bylaws set out how the PAC works. They were first passed in 1995 and last changed on September 11, 1997. Below is a short summary of each part.

The quoted text below is the 1997 wording.

Mandate, purpose, role and objectives (full text)

Mandate. To support the involvement of parents in the educational programs of McRoberts Secondary School.

Purpose. To support school staff and work together in promoting the success of students in their educational endeavours. To promote a spirit of communication, tolerance and co-operation within the school community.

Role. To provide a forum for parental discussion, involvement and participation in school activities.

Objectives, in partnership with school administrators:

  • To communicate with all partners in education.
  • To support and promote effective educational programs and pursue the goal of improving education services in a healthy, safe and supportive environment.
  • To inform parents and provide a forum about programs and services.
  • To address the concerns and needs of parents, students, and school staff and make recommendations regarding programs and services.
  • To organize volunteer programs and fundraising endeavours which support student activities.
Who is a member

Every parent or guardian of a McRoberts student is a voting member. Staff and community members without a current student can take part but do not vote.

Members are asked to come to as many meetings as possible, learn about the school's programs, and support the PAC's purpose.

The executive

The bylaws name four roles: Chair, Secretary, Treasurer and RDPA Representative. Their duties are under The 2026–27 executive, above.

Where possible, one person holds one role for no more than two years, then advises the next person.

Elections

The executive is elected at the first meeting of each school year. The previous Chair, or the principal, runs the election.

Meetings and decisions

Quorum. To make decisions, a meeting needs at least two-thirds of the executive and at least five members.

Rules. Meetings follow Robert's Rules of Order, a common set of rules for running fair meetings.

Special meetings. The principal and parents get seven days' notice of a special meeting, except when the matter is urgent.

Minutes. The Secretary records the minutes and makes them available to members.

The principal's role

The principal helps organize the PAC, encourages parents to take part, reports on school programs, and comes to meetings or sends a staff member.

Getting a copy

Every member can ask for a copy of the constitution and bylaws. Email hughmcrobertspac@gmail.com.
