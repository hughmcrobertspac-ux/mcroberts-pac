  3. School Administration Report: Mr. Leslie

    1. Ms. J. Mainville updated what happened in the Library Learning Commons (LLC) via a pre-recorded message. Gr. 8 students had their orientation at LLC, book tasting, Indigenous protocol, and so on. LLC has seen an increase in circulation of English books by 30% and French books by 60% in the last year. Reading Riot has been a great success with the financial support from PAC in the past 5 years with growing participants. (see attached LLC Year In Review). Ms. Mainville appreciates the ongoing support from the PAC and all parents.
    2. World Teacher Day was on October 5. Staff appreciated the coffee and snacks prepared by School Admin with the $200 contribution from PAC.
    3. Gr12 Grad Kick-off – T-shirt signing, Grad boat cruise on Sept 25
    4. Great turn-out on Meet the Teachers on Sept 24
    5. Over $1000 was raised on Terry Fox Run
    6. Orange shirt day on Sept 29
    7. Club days on Oct 2 during lunch hour. If students missed the sign-up opportunity, they can go to office for the list of club and information.
    8. Oct 15 is Gr 12 Assembly. First Transcript Verification Report (TVR) will go home. Students are to verify all the information. Report to school if there are any discrepancies. Final TVR is on Oct 22.
    9. Oct 21- S1 interim report will be posted
    10. Oct 23 is provincial Pro-D day. School will be closed.
    11. Nov 16- block rotation. The purpose of block rotation is to prevent students from missing the same classes throughout the semester due to sports events or other reasons.
    12. All the teaching positions are now filled. School is hiring a Noon hour supervisor.
