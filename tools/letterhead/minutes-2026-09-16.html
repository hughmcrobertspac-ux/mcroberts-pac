  1. Welcome and Introductions

    Co-Chair Nicole opened the meeting by welcoming everyone at 7:00pm.

  2. School Administration Report/ Q&A: Mr. Leslie

    1. Parents are encouraged to check on their children, ask questions, and stayed engaged. Students will do best when parents are engaged.
    2. PLT (personalized learning time) which is scheduled on Tuesdays and Thursdays allows students to see teachers for homework help or to catch up missed tests or work. Students decided what they do. Students need to sign up with the teachers or on the paper outside classrooms on the day before PLT. Teachers will take attendance. Gr. 8 and 9 students are temporarily not allowed to stay in the library in order to help them adjust to PLT.
    3. Course changes are incredibly hard this year because most classes are full and no room to move around. Changes will be finalized by the end of this week.
    4. Gr.8 wheels are a rotation of four electives that give students a taste of different electives McRoberts offers for Gr. 9 and up. Each rotation is about 4 to 5 weeks.
    5. No cell phone during classes unless for educational purposes approved by the classroom teachers. School provides wifi for student.
    6. AI use must be appropriate. Plagiarism is strictly prohibited.
    7. Final grades for all courses that are Gr.10 and higher will go on transcript. Students collect credits from these courses toward graduation requirements. Students may take courses that are higher or lower level than their current academic year.
    8. Sports Team has started. Students are encouraged to do something outside classrooms. Joining sports team or club help students stay connected with friends and create lasting memories. Universities favor well-rounded students.
    9. Our school offers AP Chemistry and AP Calculus. AP Arts is not offered this year due to low enrollment. Students may challenge the AP exam without taking the AP courses.
    10. Collaboration Day is scheduled once a month where all the teachers meet and work on a multi-subject project together.
    11. Our school district (SD38) has a $4M budget shortfall this year due to decline student enrollment.
    12. Our Theme for this year: Be Kind, Be Honest, Be Humble, Be Proud
